# Matched continuation: can the generator adapt to the shared camera frame?

Status: both GPU arms completed and their returned arrays were verified. See [SHARED_ORIENTATION_SCOPE_RETURNED_FINDINGS.md](SHARED_ORIENTATION_SCOPE_RETURNED_FINDINGS.md). Do not rerun or automatically extend these jobs. This is one short two-arm comparison, not a full-dataset run. Production `train.py`, encoders and prior diagnostic scripts are unchanged.

## Evidence and question

F13 shows that camera-oriented labels are nearly losslessly encoded, but the current adaptation scope does not reconstruct them reliably: fitted/reserved native IoU 27.81%/15.90%, common-unit pose-adjusted F-score 91.57%/77.53%. Only 18/32 fitted and 4/24 reserved predictions meet 95% bidirectional proximity. Some errors survive pose correction, with one certified all-rigid failure. Native loss still decreases, so extra exposure is a live explanation.

Question: under equal additional training exposure, does unfreezing the remaining generator components that compute shape make the camera-oriented target contract learnable? This is the planned F13 branch; no representation change or new coordinate convention is introduced.

## Two arms from the same step1000 checkpoint

| Control | Additional shape-path adaptation |
|---|---|
| Existing touch projector/embedding + full shape cross-attention and its affine norm | Same existing trainable components, plus shape self-attention, MLPs, shape input/output projections, timestep/shortcut-step embedders and shared adaptive modulation |
| Preserve Adam states, rates and step counters | Preserve those same states; start only newly unfrozen parameters with fresh Adam states |
| Projector LR 1e-4, shape CA LR 1e-5 | Same common rates; additional components LR 1e-5 |

All generator parameters start with exactly the completed run's values. Newly trainable components retain pretrained weights; no layers are reinitialized. Frozen VecSetX `.encode()` outputs, images/pointmaps, targets, decoder, conditioning interface, CFG0 and 25-step sampling are unchanged. Fixed shape positional embeddings stay buffers. No normals, sparse-point rules, target occupancy or pose tokens are added to the conditioner.

The source audit follows `mot_sparse_structure_flow.py`, `mm_latent.py`, `timestep_embedder.py`, `transformer/modulated.py` and `attention/modules.py`. Shape attends only to itself in the protected multimodal self-attention path. Layout-specific parameters remain frozen and their loss weights remain zero. Shared timestep/modulation components influence multiple branches, but the shape branch cannot read layout states. This is the trainable shape computation path, not a claim to finetune every parameter in SAM3D.

Both arms use 1000 additional updates (total2000), four fitted identities × four fitted views, batch4, BF16, zero weight decay and global gradient clipping at1. A new group-balanced 50% whole-visual dropout schedule is shared exactly. Seeds are 29+total_step, avoiding the first1000 update seeds. Three reserved views are never trained. Activation checkpointing is enabled identically in both arms to bound memory; the source uses non-reentrant checkpointing. Exact starting parameter/input hashes and numerical replay check for an unchanged initial function.

The comparison isolates the trainable-scope intervention at this learning rate/exposure. Broader scope also changes the total gradient norm under the same clipping policy; it is not a claim of an isolated capacity effect independent of optimization. Equal updates do not imply equal FLOPs or wall time. A negative result at one learning rate is not proof of insufficient capacity or architectural impossibility.

## Decision rules

Use the same target-referenced endpoints as F13: reserved mean native-target IoU≥95%, each objectmean≥90%; common-unit two-voxel P/R≥95% and no more than2pp below faithful target controls. Preserve raw fixed-frame results, known label inverse and separately measured rigid shape. Correct-minus-wrong two-voxel F-score must exceed10pp for each object under the shared procedure. Full numbers/curves remain primary; one seed/four identities do not support population inference.

- Additional adaptation succeeds while current-scope continuation does not: evidence that restricted adaptation limits this coordinate contract at the matched budget. Proceed to a bounded held-out-identity utility comparison before full training.
- Both succeed: extra exposure sufficed; prefer the simpler scope unless additional adaptation has a relevant advantage.
- Fit succeeds but reserved views fail: orientation/view transfer remains unresolved. Do not call memorization a solution.
- Both fail: record a negative for these two training treatments. Do not automatically extend again, restart VecSetX, claim arbitrary rotations impossible, or promote a full run. Reassess target-distribution/optimization explanations and the still-viable fixed-target/input-alignment route.
- Lower native loss alone, or accurate outputs without reliable correct-surface advantage, does not trigger full-run promotion.
