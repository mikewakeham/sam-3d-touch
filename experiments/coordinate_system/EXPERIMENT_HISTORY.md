# Evidence ledger: what each family contributes to the coordinate argument

Read [REPORT.md](roadmap/COORDINATE_CHECKLIST.md) for the question IDs, construction argument and stopping rules. This appendix retains negative and superseded results so that future plans cannot cite only the successful experiments. Historical handoff commands are not current instructions.

Evidence levels used here:

- **Code/math:** deduction from inspected operations, conditional on the stated input assumptions.
- **Returned measurement:** GPU-produced numbers available in reports; not a local model reproduction.
- **Artifact replay:** saved numerical arrays permit independent recalculation. Hashes establish identity, not correctness of the measuring algorithm.
- **Exploratory learning result:** a finite-task treatment; no claim of population equivalence or universal impossibility.

## External grounding, separated from local findings

The [SAM3D paper, §§2.1–2.2/C.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16624v1), models shape and layout separately, using a spatial shape latent and visual conditions. Its [pointmap ablation, E.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16624v1), reports little shape-preference change from pointmaps. This does not establish that PM is architecturally excluded from shape or that arbitrary surface conditioning should already work. The inspected local protected attention prevents shape from attending layout; layout prediction is therefore not an implicit inverse transformation for the newly added VecSetX codes. The paper is background for the architecture, not proof of the custom training recipe's coordinate correctness.

## Statistical/causal fallacy audit — 11/11 considered

| Risk | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Aggregation reversal / Simpson's paradox | Pooled native loss and per-noise/rollout endpoints differ. Keep noise regime, split and visual state separate; no claim of a proved Simpson reversal. |
| Ecological inference | Mean F-score is not a per-object guarantee; preserve object-level failures and do not interpret F-score as semantic percentage. |
| Selection / Berkson-type bias | Seeded metadata selection is preferable to selecting successful outputs. Reused development identities and tiny fitted identities still constrain generalization. No induced correlation mechanism is claimed proven. |
| Collider conditioning | Do not analyze only accurately fitted or successfully decoded samples when claiming overall utility. No explicit collider model is established here. |
| Base-rate neglect | Selected failure witnesses establish existence, not frequency over the dataset. |
| Regression to the mean | Selecting bad checkpoints/objects and then re-evaluating cannot establish an intervention effect without paired controls. |
| Survivorship | Keep empty outputs and failed runs/gates visible; missing raw artifacts are marked, not silently counted as verified. |
| Look-elsewhere | Many variants and metrics were tried. No corrected confirmatory significance or untouched success claim is made. |
| Forking paths | Record chronological changes, including gate corrections and abandoned readouts. Freeze any future primary endpoint before seeing its result. |
| Causal overreach | Matched treatments support scoped treatment effects. Oracle/camera changes rotation and normalization; stream removal changes training policy, not just frame alignment. |
| Reverse causality | Temporal order is known in interventions, but attributing poor learning to “bad features” solely because predictions are bad is circular. No such attribution is accepted. |
